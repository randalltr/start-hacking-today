## Why This Exists

I was once where you are now. I wanted to hack but had no idea where to start. Every guide listed hundreds of tools. Kali Linux felt overwhelming. I remember staring at the menu and asking friends, *“How do we even begin?”*

This book answers that question.

Instead of trying to learn everything, this guide focuses on a small workflow that produces real results. The goal is simple: get you hacking as quickly and clearly as possible.

By the end, you will understand what you are doing, why you are doing it, and how real hacking workflows fit together.

---

## The Philosophy

Most beginners struggle because of tool overload. Professional hackers succeed because they understand process.

This book intentionally limits you to three tools:

- **Nmap** — discover the attack surface
- **ExploitDB** — find working exploits
- **Burp Suite** — understand and manipulate web applications

Plus one bonus tool:

- **tmux** — work efficiently like a real hacker

Constraint creates clarity. Clarity creates progress.
